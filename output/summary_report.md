1. **Lean into festival season**: stock and promote top categories (especially electronics) before Oct–Nov to capture peak demand.
2. **Fix the margin drag**: renegotiate or reprice the lowest-margin category and tighten deep-discount thresholds.
3. **Expand the lagging region**: targeted campaigns and logistics investment in the weakest region mirroring the leader's playbook.
4. **Double down on best sellers**: feature the top products in bundles and cross-sells to lift average order value.
